# Currency & Rewards

Twelve illustrated pickup and reward sprites on transparent backgrounds. Each individual PNG is 160 × 160 pixels. Copy the sprites you need from `sprites/` into your cart's assets and list them in `game.json`. The four gems use distinct outlines in addition to color.

![Currency and Rewards sprite preview with a 32-pixel size check](preview.png)

The transparent [sprite sheet](sheet.png) is 640 × 480 pixels with 160 × 160 cells. The cell names match the individual PNG filenames.

| Row | Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | coin | coin-stack | coin-pouch | ruby |
| 2 | sapphire | emerald | amethyst | star |
| 3 | trophy | gift | treasure-chest | medal |

Original generated artwork for NOVA Mobile.
