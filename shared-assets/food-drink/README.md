# Food & Drink

Sixteen illustrated sprites for produce, pantry staples, meals, and treats. Each individual PNG is 160 × 160 pixels with a transparent background. Copy the sprites you need from `sprites/` into your cart's assets and list them in `game.json`.

![Food and Drink sprite preview with a 32-pixel size check](preview.png)

The transparent [sprite sheet](sheet.png) is 640 × 640 pixels with 160 × 160 cells. The cell names match the individual PNG filenames.

| Row | Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | apple | bananas | strawberry | blueberries |
| 2 | carrot | broccoli | tomato | mushroom |
| 3 | bread-loaf | croissant | cheese | egg |
| 4 | pizza-slice | soup | cupcake | orange-juice |

Original generated artwork for NOVA Mobile.
