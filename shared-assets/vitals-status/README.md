# Vitals & Status

Twelve illustrated sprites on transparent backgrounds for health, energy, stamina, and status effects. Each PNG is 160 × 160 pixels. Copy individual sprites from `sprites/` into your cart's assets and list them in `game.json`.

![Vitals and Status sprite preview with a 32-pixel size check](preview.png)

The transparent [sprite sheet](sheet.png) is 640 × 480 pixels with 160 × 160 cells. The cell names match the individual PNG filenames.

| Row | Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | heart-full | heart-half | heart-empty | shield |
| 2 | energy | stamina | health-potion | mana-potion |
| 3 | buff | debuff | status-empty | status-full |

Original generated artwork for NOVA Mobile.
