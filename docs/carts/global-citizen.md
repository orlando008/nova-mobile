# Global Citizen

Global Citizen is a portrait world geography game. Choose the world or one of six regions, then play a ten-question quiz. Each round draws distinct countries from the selected region. Choosing an answer reveals whether it was correct and shows the answer before the next question. The results show the score and offer a new round or a return to the quiz menu.

| Quiz | How to play |
| --- | --- |
| Countries | Tap the requested country or its dot on the map. Drag to pan. Pinch or use the zoom buttons to zoom, and use the reset button to restore the original view. Tab through visible countries and press Enter or Space to select one from a keyboard. |
| Country Silhouette | Identify an unlabeled country outline from three names. Outlines are scaled to fit and shown north-up. France uses its metropolitan outline. |
| Flags | Identify the country represented by a flag from three options. |
| Capitals | Choose the capital from three options. Prompts identify the government function where a country uses multiple capitals or an administrative center. |

The question bank covers the 193 UN member states and the two UN observer states. Other areas appear on the map for context. The same seed, quiz, and region reproduce a round. Flags and map data are served with the game.

[Country boundaries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/) and [capital city records](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-populated-places/) are based on the [public-domain Natural Earth dataset](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) (snapshot `ca96624`). The game simplifies outlines for mobile use. Boundaries follow Natural Earth's depiction of locations and areas of control. Country and capital names use common English forms where applicable. The capital list includes updates such as [Ciudad de la Paz](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/country/equatorialguinea). The flag atlas uses [country-flags](https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags/tree/c09927e63705529bbf59ca6684cd9b23225dddad), which sources flag artwork from Wikimedia Commons. The globe illustration was generated for this game.
