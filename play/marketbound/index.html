      How to play

      1. Swipe a piece or tap two neighbors to make a row or column of three matching pieces.
      2. Fill the goals before you run out of moves. A swap that makes no match is free.
      3. From level 2, a match of four or more clears its whole row or column.
      4. Clear produce over brown soil to dig up the plots. Finish with moves to spare to earn more stars.
