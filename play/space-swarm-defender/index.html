How to play
  1. Choose a tower at the bottom, then tap an empty hex pad. Pulse hits single targets, Nova hits groups, and Cryo slows the swarm.
  2. Tap an installed tower to see its range, upgrade it, or salvage it for credits.
  3. Launch each wave when your defenses are ready. Survive eight waves, including the queen’s assault, to secure the sector.
